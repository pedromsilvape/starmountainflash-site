Who processes your data

The controller of the data collected on this website is Pedro Miguel da Silva Pereira, trading under the business name Starmountain Flash, based in Covilhã, Portugal, tax number (NIF) 251504387.

For any privacy question, write to geral@starmountainflash.pt or call +351 937 800 553.

What data is collected

Data is only collected when visitors send it themselves, or when they expressly allow audience measurement.

Form and quote request

When you send a request through the website, the following is stored:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Company, if you provide it
  • Phone number, if you provide it
  • Service requested
  • The message you write
  • The page from which the request was sent, and the date and time
  • The page through which you entered the website and where you came from (for example, a search engine, a social network or a campaign), when that information exists. It is kept in the browser only during the visit and only reaches us if you send the request.
  • A code derived from the IP address, obtained through a cryptographic hash function with a secret. This code is used only to limit repeated submissions and cannot be used to reconstruct the IP address.

Audience measurement

The cookie notice has two categories, which you can accept separately. If you accept Measurement, Google Analytics 4 and Microsoft Clarity are loaded. Clarity shows, in aggregate, where people click and how far they scroll, and records navigation on the pages, with the text typed into forms hidden. If you accept Marketing, the Meta Pixel is loaded, to measure whether visits from Meta ads (Facebook and Instagram) lead to requests. If you decline, none of this code is loaded and the website works just the same. Your choice is stored only in your browser and can be changed at any time under Cookie preferences, in the footer.

The website also uses Plausible, which counts visits without cookies and without storing data that could identify visitors.

Technical logs

The hosting provider (Netlify) keeps access logs for security and operational reasons, like any web server. They are not used for profiling.

What it is used for and on what legal basis

  • Replying to your request and preparing a proposal. Legal basis: steps taken at your request prior to entering into a contract, under Article 6(1)(b) of the GDPR.
  • Preventing automated submissions and abuse of the form. Legal basis: legitimate interest in keeping the service available, Article 6(1)(f).
  • Understanding which pages are useful. Legal basis: your consent, Article 6(1)(a), given in the cookie notice and revocable at any time.

How long it is kept

  • Requests that did not lead to a contract: up to 2 years from the last contact, so that the conversation can be resumed.
  • Requests that led to a contract: for the period required by tax and commercial law, currently 10 years.
  • Code derived from the IP address: deleted after 30 days.
  • Audience data: according to the retention configured in Google Analytics, up to a maximum of 14 months; in Microsoft Clarity, recordings and heatmaps remain available for the period set by Microsoft.

Who it is shared with

The data is not sold or passed on for advertising purposes. It is processed by the following providers, who act as processors and only on instructions:

  • Netlify: website hosting. Data may be processed outside the European Union, under the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.
  • Supabase: database where requests are stored.
  • Brevo: sending the email notification for each new request. A company based in the European Union.
  • Google Ireland: Google Analytics, only if you accept the Measurement category.
  • Microsoft Ireland: Microsoft Clarity, only if you accept the Measurement category.
  • Meta Platforms Ireland: Meta Pixel, only if you accept the Marketing category.
  • Plausible Insights: cookieless visit counting. A company based in the European Union.

Your rights

You may at any time request access to, rectification, erasure or restriction of your data, object to certain processing, request data portability and withdraw any consent you have given, without affecting the lawfulness of processing carried out before withdrawal.

To exercise any of these rights, simply write to geral@starmountainflash.pt. A reply is given within one month.

If you believe your data is not being processed correctly, you may lodge a complaint with the Portuguese Data Protection Authority (Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados, CNPD) .

Cookies and local storage

The website does not use cookies to work. Only these mechanisms are used:

  • Browser storage, to store your answer to the cookie notice and, only during the visit, the entry page and where you came from. It is not sent to any server unless you send a request.
  • Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity cookies, created only if you accept the Measurement category.
  • Meta Pixel cookie, created only if you accept the Marketing category.

To change your choice, click Cookie preferences in the footer. Details of each cookie, with names and durations, are in the cookie policy.

Security

The website is served exclusively over an encrypted connection (HTTPS). The access keys to the database and to the email service exist only on the server and never reach the browser. Access to stored requests is limited to the controller.

Minors

Starmountain Flash's services are aimed at businesses and professionals. The website is not intended for anyone under 16 and does not knowingly collect data from minors.

If you become aware that a minor has sent us personal data without the authorisation of the holder of parental responsibility, write to geral@starmountainflash.pt and that data will be deleted.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated whenever the services or the law change. The date of the last update is shown alongside.

See also the cookie policy, the legal notice and the terms and conditions.
