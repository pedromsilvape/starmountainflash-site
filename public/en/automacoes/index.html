01 · Examples

What can be automated,
before and after.

If someone at the company spends hours each week copying data from one place to another, answering the same questions or filling in sheets by hand, there's a good chance that work can run on its own.

Customer service

Repeated questions answered

Before

You answer the same questions about opening hours, prices and availability by hand.

After

Frequent questions get an instant answer. Only the others reach you.

Requests

Contacts that don't get lost

Before

Requests arrive by email, form and phone, and some get forgotten.

After

Every request goes into the CRM with its source and date, and gets an automatic reply.

Documents

Invoices without copy and paste

Before

The details of each invoice are copied one by one into Excel.

After

The documents are read and the data goes into the sheet on its own.

Reports

The Monday report

Before

Every week someone pulls numbers from several sheets to put the report together.

After

The report is generated and sent on its own, always at the same time.

Bookings

Fewer missed appointments

Before

Client reminders are sent by hand, when there's time.

After

Each client gets an automatic confirmation and reminder.

Integrations

Tools that talk to each other

Before

The same information is typed in three different places.

After

You type it once and the other tools update themselves.

New clients

Automatic client onboarding

Before

Each new client means creating folders, records and tasks by hand.

After

Everything is created the moment the client comes in.

Portals

Portals without integration

Before

Documents downloaded one by one from portals that don't connect to anything.

After

A bot logs into the portal, downloads the documents and files them for you.

Each automation is designed around your process and the tools you already use.

04 · FAQ

Automations,
in a few words.

Do I need a website to automate?

No. Automations work with the tools you already use: email, spreadsheets, CRM, management software. The website is just one of the possible connections.

How much does an automation cost?

A simple automation costs between €800 and €1,200; with AI or two to three flows, between €1,200 and €2,500; a custom system starts at €3,500. The exact price goes in the proposal, after the survey. I explain what makes the price vary in how much an AI automation costs.

Is my data safe?

Automations use the company's own accounts and tools, with access only to what they need to work.

What if something goes wrong?

Automations are tested with real cases before they go live. How follow-up and adjustments work is set out in the proposal.

Where does artificial intelligence come in?

In tasks that involve text: sorting emails, summarising documents, extracting data or drafting replies. When it matters, there's always a human review before anything goes out.
