04 · FAQ

What people
ask me most.

Does Google allow asking for reviews?

It does, as long as every client is asked and nothing is given in return. That's how the system is set up.

Can you delete a negative review?

No. Only Google removes reviews, and only those that break its rules, such as spam, offensive content or reviews from people who were never clients. In those cases I file the removal request for you.

Will the replies sound like a robot wrote them?

Before starting, we pick together some replies you've given or would like to give, and the AI writes from those. The first weeks all go through you.

Does the team have to do anything?

No. The request goes out from bookings, invoices or the client list. At the counter, the QR code on display is enough.

Do I need to buy any software?

You don't need to install anything. I work with the Google profile you already have.

What kind of business is it for?

For businesses where clients choose on Google Maps: clinics, garages, restaurants, beauty salons, gyms, technical services and shops.
