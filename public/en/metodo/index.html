01 · In detail

Phase by phase. No small print.

You always know where the project stands, what comes next and what's still needed from you.

01

Reconnaissance

Before designing anything, understanding the business, its customers and where time or opportunities are being lost.

What happens

  • A conversation about goals and priorities
  • A review of the current site, if there is one
  • A look at the competition and the tools you use

What you get

  • A free visual proposal
  • If you want to go ahead, a quote tailored to what you asked for
  • A recommendation on the right format: landing page, website, store or automation

What I need from you

  • A conversation of about 30 minutes, online
  • Access to what already exists: website, social media, materials
02

Execution

Once the quote is approved, the project moves ahead on the agreed timeline, with review points set from the start.

What happens

  • Custom design, with a first review
  • Development in code, with a second review
  • Mobile, speed and SEO testing
  • Launch and domain set-up

What you get

  • The site live, fast and ready for Google and AI
  • Logins and an explanation of how it works

What I need from you

  • Basic texts about the business
  • Logo and photos, if you have them
  • Feedback at the review points, on the agreed dates
03

Automation

After launch, or as a separate project, the business's repetitive work starts running on its own.

What happens

  • Mapping repetitive tasks and the time they take
  • Building the automation with your tools
  • Testing with real cases before it goes live

What you get

  • The automation up and running
  • A simple explanation of how it works
  • Follow-up in the first few weeks

What I need from you

  • Access to the tools involved
  • Someone who knows the process well

04 · FAQ

About the method, in a few words.

What if I want to change something halfway?+

That's what the review points are for: changes requested at those moments are part of the project. Changes that alter the agreed scope are discussed first and, if needed, quoted separately.

How many rounds of changes are included?+

They're set out in the quote, depending on the type of project, so we both know from the start what to expect.

How do payments work?+

The terms are in the quote. The most common arrangement is to split payment between the start and the delivery of the project.

How long does a project take?+

It depends on the format. The timeline is agreed before we start. Landing pages are the quickest; online stores and automations take longer.

Are meetings in person or online?+

Online, which lets me work with companies all over the country. I'm based in Covilhã and, where it makes sense, we can also meet in person.

What if the project doesn't go ahead after the proposal?+

Nothing. The visual proposal is free and is there precisely to see whether it makes sense for both of us. We only go ahead if you want to, after the quote.
