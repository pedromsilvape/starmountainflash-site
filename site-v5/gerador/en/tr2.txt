639|06 · FAQ
640|What is RAG?
641|RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) is the technique where the assistant first looks for the right excerpts in the company's documents and only then writes the answer based on them. That's what lets it cite the source instead of answering from memory.
642|Do the documents leave the company?
643|It depends on the option chosen in the survey: everything on the company's servers, or in a cloud with a business contract that doesn't use the data to train models. I explain the differences before you decide.
644|Can the AI make up answers?
645|It can get things wrong, like any system. That's why every answer shows the source document and the assistant is set up to say when it finds nothing.
646|Will an intern see management's contracts?
647|No. The assistant follows the permissions that already exist on the folders. Anyone without access to a document gets no answers based on it.
648|Which document types does it accept?
649|PDF, Word, Excel, PowerPoint, internal pages and, where it makes sense, emails. Scanned documents too, once converted to text.
